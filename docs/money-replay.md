# Money Replay

The colourful **Your cycle, replayed** card at the top of Insights opens a
manually paced, keyboard-accessible story. Chapters show recorded net spending,
a repeat merchant, the leading category, small purchases, a standout day, the
leading payment source, a comparable category change and posted refunds. Chapters
appear only when their supporting data exists. There is no timer or autoplay.

Every chapter opens its original transaction IDs. Comparisons have separate
current/previous-cycle tabs, so their difference is not confused with a sum of
both periods. Escape returns from evidence to the same chapter. Reduced-motion
preferences suppress chapter movement. Hidden balances mask monetary figures;
decorative colours and progress segments do not encode amounts. Merchant names,
dates and counts remain visible under the existing privacy convention.

The existing spending, pay-cycle, refund and payment-source engines provide the
facts. Refunds remain on their received dates and inherit linked purchase metadata.
Income, investments, card repayments and balance adjustments are excluded. Full
cycle category totals are separate from equal-prefix comparisons. Archived account
types remain known, and missing merchants/accounts stay unknown. Repeat merchants
are ranked by actual purchase count; equal repeated payments retain distinct IDs.
Small purchases means strictly below ₹150 each and uses a labelled gross basis
before refunds. Other chapters use net spending except the explicit refund total
and category difference. Future entries and verified future clocks are excluded.

This is a presentation of recorded facts, not a spending judgment, a claimed
no-spend streak, a visited-location inference, a bank-coverage guarantee or an
AI-generated financial recommendation. No budget, sharing setup, tracking, paid
service, schema change or financial write is introduced. Home is unchanged.
